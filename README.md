## Key Features

### 1. AI English Tutor
- Practice English conversation with a real AI (Google Gemini).
- Setup: Get a free API key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/), and add it to `.env.local` as `GEMINI_API_KEY`.
- Navigate to `/practice` and use your microphone to speak.

### 2. Hand Gesture Control
- Control the mouse cursor using your hand via webcam.
- **Enable**: Click the "Hand" icon in the top right of the navbar.
- **Move**: Move your index finger to move the cursor.
- **Click**: Pinch your thumb and index finger together to click.
